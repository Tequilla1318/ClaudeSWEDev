Options Checker

See where your trading money actually goes

Most traders lose money to costs, overpriced options, bad timing and oversized bets, not to bad luck. These tools measure those losses. They run entirely in your browser.

  • Trade-log report card

    Drop in your Interactive Brokers trades file. See what commissions took, how much you traded, and which costly habits from the research show up in your trades.

  • Risk-of-ruin calculator

    Enter your win rate, average win and loss, and how much you risk per trade. See how often that sizing leads to a deep drawdown, and how it compares with the Kelly fraction.

  • Premium-selling backtester

    What popular SPX put-selling rules did through 2008, 2018, 2020 and 2022, after costs, next to holding the index.

  • Pre-trade option checker

    The spread, break-even and event risk of a trade before you place it. Planned
Educational tools, not financial advice. Open source: projects/options-checker.
