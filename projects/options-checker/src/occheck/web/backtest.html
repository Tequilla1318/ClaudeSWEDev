    How far to trust this

    • Option prices are modelled, not historical quotes. The model was checked against the real Cboe PutWrite index for at-the-money puts: validation report.
    • Out-of-the-money puts are priced with a modest volatility skew, checked against Cboe's 2% out-of-the-money PutWrite index from 1990 to today: skew calibration. Today's real SPX skew is about three times steeper than that historical fit, so far out-of-the-money rules likely collect more premium in today's market than shown here.
    • Fills happen at the daily close with a fixed cost. In a crash, real spreads widen and fills get worse.
    Educational tool, not financial advice. Past results, real or simulated, don't predict future ones.
