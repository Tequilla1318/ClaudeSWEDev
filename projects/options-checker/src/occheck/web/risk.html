Options Checker

Risk-of-ruin calculator

The same strategy can survive or blow up depending only on how much you risk per trade. This runs 10,000 simulated runs of your trades and shows the spread of outcomes.

"Risk per trade" is what you lose on an average losing trade, as a share of the account at that moment. For a cash-secured put, that is the realistic loss, not the whole collateral.

    Assumes trades are independent and your win rate and average win and loss stay the same. Real trades cluster: losses often arrive together in a crash, so real risk is higher than this. Research: Kelly criterion, gambler's ruin.

    Educational tool, not financial advice.
