Size it by the break loss

    How the odds are worked out. If the target is worth the offer when the deal closes and the break price when it fails, then today's price = p × offer + (1 − p) × break price. Solving for p gives the market-implied odds. It ignores interest and the time value of money, which is the usual quick read on a deal.

    What it leaves out. The short acquirer leg's gain or loss if a stock deal breaks, competing bids, collars, CVRs, margin interest, and taxes. A break can also gap below your break-price guess.

    Educational tool, not financial advice. It doesn't place trades or say which deals to buy.
